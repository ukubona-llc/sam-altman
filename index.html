2. Altman thesis

Pro view (Gemini summary)

  • Chief architect of the generative AI boom
  • Blunt about risks and energy demand
  • Steady under pressure

Critical view (the branch you chose)

  • Slick marketer masking commercialization
  • Governance and transparency concerns
  • Face of job, copyright, energy, concentration anxiety

Your position

  • Not likeable, not technically adept, but his opportunism created the gold rush: A+ as a libertarian.
  • Scaling means compute means money; the Microsoft/Azure deal came around GPT-3.
  • Sutskever: purest, SSI releases nothing until ready, no longer market-relevant. Amodei: narrative doesn't match his compute scaling.
  • Willing to be disproved, which would still be a free-market move.

Critique's three propositions: (1) Altman isn't the technical originator plausible; (2) he's unusually effective at assembling support and financing plausible, most interesting; (3) success proves optimality, tricked counterparties, or safety-as-theater does not follow.
